# Platform notes

Last reviewed: 2026-11. Platforms change limits and feed behavior often. Treat the numbers
below as a starting point and confirm in the platform's own help pages before relying on them.

| Platform | Text limit (approx.) | What works | Links |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 3,000 characters per post | Text posts with a strong first 1-2 lines (the rest hides behind "see more"); document/PDF carousels for frameworks; personal stories from founders and team | Many marketers put links in the first comment or at the end; test both |
| X | 280 characters on free accounts, longer for paid accounts | One sharp claim per post; threads for step-by-step content; replies and quote posts drive reach | Link in the last post of a thread or in a reply |
| Instagram | 2,200 characters per caption | Carousels and Reels; the first line of the caption shows before truncation; text on images must be readable on a phone | Captions don't make links clickable; use the bio or stories |
| Threads | 500 characters per post | Conversational, quick takes, replies | Links allowed, keep them secondary |
| Bluesky | 300 characters per post | Conversational, threads, niche communities | Link cards supported |
| Facebook | Long posts allowed | Community and local audiences; native video | Links allowed |
| TikTok / Reels / Shorts | Video, with short captions | Hook in the first 2-3 seconds; captions on screen; one idea per video; vertical 9:16 | Bio link |

## Short video script format

```text
HOOK (0-3s):   [What's said + on-screen text]
SETUP (3-10s): [The problem or context]
PAYOFF:        [The insight, steps, or reveal]
CTA (last 3s): [One action]
VISUAL NOTES:  [B-roll, captions, keep key text away from the edges and bottom
                where the app's buttons and captions sit]
```

## Carousel / document format

```text
Slide 1: Hook headline (promise or claim), 8 words or fewer
Slides 2-N: One point per slide, short headline + 1-2 lines
Last slide: Summary + call to action
```
