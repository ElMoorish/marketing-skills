## 1. Company and product

- **Brand / product name:** Ledgerly (one word, capital L only)
- **One-line description:** Bookkeeping software that closes the month for small agencies in a day.
- **What it does:** Ledgerly connects to the agency's bank, card and invoicing tools, matches
  transactions to client projects automatically, and produces a month-end report the
  accountant can sign off.
- **Category buyers search for:** small business bookkeeping software
- **Business model:** B2B SaaS
- **Pricing:** Starter $39/mo, Team $99/mo (up to 10 users). 14-day free trial, no card required.
- **Website:** https://ledgerly.example
- **Markets and regulations:** US and UK. FTC and ASA advertising rules; GDPR for UK customers.

## 2. Ideal customer

- **Who they are:** Founder or operations lead at a creative or marketing agency
- **Company shape:** 5-40 people, $1-10M revenue, project-based billing
- **Trigger event:** The accountant sends back last month's books with questions, again.
- **What they use today:** A spreadsheet plus generic accounting software, or a part-time bookkeeper
- **Who decides:** The founder, usually within 2 weeks of the trial
- **Do NOT target:** Freelancers working alone (high churn), companies over 100 people (need ERP)

## 3. Positioning

- **Real alternatives:** Generic accounting software, a part-time bookkeeper, spreadsheets
- **What only we have:** Matching transactions to client projects, not just categories
- **Value themes:** Project-level matching -> know which clients are profitable; auto month-end report -> close in a day
- **Positioning statement:** For agency owners who lose days every month reconciling books,
  Ledgerly is bookkeeping software that closes the month in a day. Unlike generic
  accounting tools, we match every transaction to the client project it belongs to.

## 4. Customer pains and objections

- **Push:** Month-end takes 3-4 days; no idea which clients make money
- **Pull:** One-day close; profit by client
- **Habit:** "Our spreadsheet works well enough"
- **Anxiety:** Migrating historical data; the accountant not accepting the reports
- **Objections:**
  - "We already pay for accounting software" -> Ledgerly sits on top of it and syncs both ways (P-02)
  - "My accountant won't use it" -> Reports export in the accountant's existing format (P-03)

## 5. Customer language bank

- Problem: "I spend the first week of every month playing detective with receipts." (Interview, 2026-10-14)
- Result: "For the first time I know which clients are actually worth keeping." (G2 review, 2026-09-02)
- **Words to avoid:** "seamless", "revolutionary", "AI-powered" (customers told us they tune it out)

## 8. Brand voice

- **Voice traits:**
  - Plain: means short sentences and real numbers; does NOT mean dull.
  - Warm: means we sound like a helpful peer; does NOT mean jokes in every line.
  - Confident: means we state what we do well; does NOT mean attacking competitors.
- **Tone by channel:** Website = clear and calm. LinkedIn = founder voice, first person. Email = brief and friendly.
- **Writing samples we love:**
  1. "Month-end shouldn't be a week. Ledgerly gets it done by Friday lunch."
  2. "We built Ledgerly after our own agency spent 40 hours a month on bookkeeping. Here's what we changed."
- **Never sound like this:** "Unlock seamless financial synergy with our AI-powered platform." — vague, buzzwords, says nothing.
- **Formatting rules:** US spelling. No emoji on the website; one max on social. Oxford comma yes.

## 9. Channels and goals
